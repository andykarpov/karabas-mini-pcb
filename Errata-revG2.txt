ERRATA revG2:

1) SDRAM U11 pin 19 (~CS) is wired to FPGA U7 pin T3 (net ~{SDR_CS}). On the earlier revisions T3 is
~{CONF_CS} and SDRAM ~CS is not connected to the FPGA, so the cores treat T3 as the flash chip select and
drive it high (FLASH_CS_N = 1): the SDRAM is never selected and every core that uses it fails. Only
karabas-go-core-sdram-test drives T3 as SDRAM ~CS.

The net has no pull resistor, and the track is on the top layer only, without vias.

Found in the schematic and the PCB files, not checked on a revG2 board.

Hotfix: cut the ~{SDR_CS} track next to U11 pin 19 and connect pin 19 to GND through a 1k..10k resistor
or a wire. A pull-down alone does not work: the FPGA output keeps the pin high.

Software fix (needs a rebuild of each core): drive T3 low in the Mini G tops (FLASH_CS_N = 0). It is safe
on revG too: the config flash footprint is not populated and no core uses it.
Not needed together with the hotfix.

Next revision: connect U11 pin 19 to GND.
